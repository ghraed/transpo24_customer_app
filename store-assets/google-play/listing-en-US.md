## Short description

## Full description

Transpo24 connects customers who need something transported with independent transport providers.

Create a transport request in a few steps:

- Choose the type of transport you need.
- Set the pickup and delivery locations.
- Add the relevant item details and photos.
- Receive and compare offers from transport providers.
- Select an offer and follow the request's progress.
- Manage requests, payments and account settings in one place.

Transpo24 supports transport requests for vehicles, motorcycles, goods and furniture. Location features help you choose accurate pickup and delivery points, while notifications keep you informed about important request updates.

Transport availability, supported payment methods and individual features may vary by location.

Transpo24 is a technology marketplace. Physical transport services are provided by independent transport providers.
